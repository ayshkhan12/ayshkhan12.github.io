About

Hi, I’m Aysh

I’m a Computer Science graduate from the University of Hull with First Class Honours, and I first trained as an engineer in Hong Kong. I like building things people actually use: secure APIs, web apps, a mobile app for finding halal food, and a rota tool for my colleagues at work.

In my own time I chase a bigger question: can an AI agent learn to live in a world the way animals do, driven by hunger, fear and curiosity instead of one handwritten goal? That question has taken me from tabular Q learning to world models, episodic memory and a “global workspace”. You can meet the simplest version of it on the Lab page.

Three years of customer facing work in Hong Kong, London and Hull taught me the other half of engineering: listening, staying calm when things break, and explaining things clearly. I’ve tutored children, led student teams and been Employee of the Month, and I want to bring all of that to a team that builds things people rely on.
