§01 Self-model

Software that helps.
Agents that learn.

I’m Aysh, a Computer Science graduate from the University of Hull (First Class Honours), first trained as an engineer in Hong Kong. I like building things people actually use: secure APIs, web apps, a booking site for a local business, and a rota tool for my colleagues at work.

In my own time I chase a bigger question: can an AI agent learn to live in a world the way animals do, driven by hunger, fear and curiosity instead of one hand-written goal? That question has taken me from tabular Q-learning to world models, episodic memory and a “global workspace”, and it’s why there’s a creature living at the top of this page.

Three years of customer-facing work in Hong Kong, London and Hull taught me the other half of engineering: listening, staying calm when things break, and explaining things clearly. I’ve tutored children, led student teams and been Employee of the Month, and I want to bring all of that to a team that builds things people rely on.
